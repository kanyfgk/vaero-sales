Privacy Policy

VAERO collects only the information needed to provide, improve and support the experience.

Basic usage data may be used to understand page visits, support requests, order interest and delivery-related actions.

Personal information is not sold. Data is used only for VAERO’s service, support and improvement process.

Terms of Use

By using VAERO pages, tracking tools or support links, you agree to use them for their intended purpose.

Product information, availability and delivery details may change depending on current stock and active batches.

Final order details are confirmed directly through VAERO support before completion.

Shipping & Tracking

VAERO products are prepared and shipped according to available batch status.

When an order is confirmed, delivery timing and tracking information are shared as clearly as possible.

Tracking tools are provided to help customers follow their order status after dispatch.

Returns & Refunds

If the product arrives damaged or does not match the confirmed order details, support should be contacted as soon as possible.

Return or replacement options are reviewed based on the condition of the product and the order case.

Used, damaged or altered products may not be eligible for return.

VAERO Care

VAERO Care is VAERO’s after-sales support approach, created so customers are not left alone after purchase.

This support may include usage guidance, setup direction, care recommendations, scent and atmosphere advice, and the review of product-related issues.

Every support request is reviewed individually. When suitable, repair, part support, usage guidance or replacement options may be evaluated depending on the case.

VAERO Care is not an unlimited warranty. User error, impact, dropping, incorrect use, leakage, placing the device upside down, unauthorized intervention or the use of different oils or scents are reviewed separately and may not be covered.

In suitable cases, first chamber support may be considered within VAERO Care as a goodwill-based support option. This is not an automatic right or guaranteed coverage.

For every support request, customers can contact VAERO directly through WhatsApp.

Need support?

For order questions, delivery details, product support, VAERO Care requests or batch availability, you can contact VAERO directly.
